Side panel redesign concepts

Four directions for the hover/pin panel on the tree page. Each keeps every current piece of behaviour: the 2s dwell indicator that fills while you hover a person, pinning so it stays open once dwelled (with a way to close it again), the photo, name and note, the three actions (Person Profile / Focus / Add Offspring), and the gallery. Click "Preview dwell → pin" on any screen to see the fill-and-pin motion play out.

01 — Floating Card

The panel lifts off the screen edge into a detached, rounded card with margin all around. The avatar becomes a badge overlapping the top edge, with the dwell timer drawn as a ring around it instead of a bar. Actions read as a clean vertical stack of rows; the gallery becomes a horizontal filmstrip so it never fights the actions for height.

02 — Compact Rail → Expand

A slim 64px rail sits at the edge at all times, so the page never feels empty-left when no one's hovered. Hovering a node lights a small avatar chip on the rail with the dwell ring around it; dwelling expands the rail outward into the full panel in place, rather than sliding a whole new panel over the canvas.

03 — Tabbed Drawer

Keeps today's fixed edge-drawer shape, but compresses the header into one row (avatar + name/note side by side) and splits Actions and Gallery into a segmented switch below it. Good for people with a lot of photos — the gallery gets its own full-height scroll area instead of competing with the action list for room.

04 — Contextual Popover

Instead of a fixed spot on the screen edge, the panel detaches and anchors right next to whichever bubble is hovered, with a small pointer tail connecting them — so the connection between panel and person stays obvious even while panning. Dwelling pins it in place with a close button; it's deliberately the most compact of the four, trading gallery size for staying out of the way of the tree itself.
