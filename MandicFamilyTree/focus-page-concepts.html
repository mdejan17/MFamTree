Mandić Family Tree — Focus page

Five directions for a Focus page that doesn't feel like the Tree page

The Home tree is a pannable org-chart: right-angled dashed connectors, uniform rows. Focus is meant to be a close-up on one person and their immediate family — so none of these five reuse that diagram language. Content below is the real shape of a record (an empty parents slot, a married couple, five children) so you can judge each layout against what you'll actually see.

The couple sits at the center of gravity; parents arc in above, children fan out below along curved orbit paths. Reads as "this person's solar system," not a chart.
